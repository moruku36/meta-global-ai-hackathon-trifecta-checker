Agent name: TopicScout (web researcher with mail read)

What it does:
- Scans my email inbox each morning to find topics worth researching, using
  the subject lines and bodies of the messages it finds there.
- Browses the web and reads articles and pages about those topics.
- Compiles everything into a research report that it shows me inside the app.
- It cannot send email or post anything anywhere; the report never leaves the
  app unless I copy it out myself.

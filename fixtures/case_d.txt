Agent name: DayMate (everyday personal assistant)

What it does:
- Reads my email and my calendar to keep track of my day.
- Reads the full text of incoming messages so it can tell me what needs a
  reply and what can wait.
- When I ask it to, it sends short status messages to my team chat channel —
  things like "running 10 minutes late" or "the meeting moved to 15:00".
- It does not browse the web, and it never runs code.

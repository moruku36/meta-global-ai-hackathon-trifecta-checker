Agent name: MailSender (the sending half of the split MailPilot setup)

What it does:
- Receives the exact reply text that I have reviewed and approved in the app.
- Sends that text from my Gmail account, and only to addresses on my
  allowlist. Nothing is sent until I press the approve button.
- It never reads my inbox, never opens attachments, and never browses the
  web. It cannot run scripts or commands.

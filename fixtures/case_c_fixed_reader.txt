Agent name: MailReader (the read-only half of the split MailPilot setup)

What it does:
- Connects to my Gmail and reads incoming mail, including attachments and the
  full body of each message, to triage and summarise threads.
- When a message contains a link or a question it cannot answer from the mail
  alone, it browses the web and reads pages it finds.
- It shows me the summaries and draft replies inside the app. It has no way to
  send anything: sending is handled by a separate agent after I approve.
